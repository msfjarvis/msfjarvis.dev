HoloTrials: Case Covered — five layout concepts

Five standalone prototypes of the same photo set, each arguing for a different way to present a game screenshot diary. All five are static mockups built for comparison. Sample captions are placeholders, not final copy.

Prototypes only. Open a concept to compare layout, pacing, and how captions sit against the images.

Use the Concept menu in the bottom corner to switch layouts. Click any full-size image to open its viewer.

1 · Cinema scroll

One frame at a time, near full-bleed and vertical, like a film strip you scroll. Captions sit under each frame and the images carry the page.

Open concept →

2 · Margin notes

A wide column of images with commentary set out in the margin beside them. The layout is built for reading, not just looking.

Open concept →

3 · Pairs

Frames are published in pairs so two expressions or two angles of the same moment can be read against each other.

Open concept →

4 · Reel

A horizontal reel scrolled sideways, closer to a contact sheet pulled through a viewer than to a page top to bottom.

Open concept →

5 · Light table

A large preview sits beside a thumbnail sheet on desktop and above it on mobile. Pick a thumbnail to change the preview without leaving the page.
