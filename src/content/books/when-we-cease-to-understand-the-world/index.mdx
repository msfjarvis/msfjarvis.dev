Somewhat weird, the mixing of historical events and personalities with increasing amounts of fiction didn't really jibe with me. The trope of making out every physicist and mathematician into a deeply paranoid we-are-engineering-our-demise doomer got old quickly. I know the geniuses weren't exactly all stable people, but making each one of them as a mentally ill loner is not particularly engaging either. I'd pass on it.
