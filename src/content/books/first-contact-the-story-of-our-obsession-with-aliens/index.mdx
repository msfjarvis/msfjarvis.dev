Becky Ferreira has gone above and beyond in the research of this book and it reflects very well in the content. The book covers many diverse viewpoints on the topic of finding life in the cosmos and does justice to all of them. The history of UFO encounters and the investigations behind the reality behind some of them was quite entertaining to read, and I learned a lot of things over the course of this book. This was all so new and interesting to me that I had to go find how to create bookmarks and annotations in [KOReader](https://koreader.rocks) just so I could keep referring back to this book.
