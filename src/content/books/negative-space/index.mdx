Probably one of the most intellectually challenging books I've read. Lots of drug abuse and body horror, but very well written and complex characters going through seemingly endless suffering and still managing to briefly steal some moments of joy. Despite the very dark setting it was never difficult to keep reading it, and I was hooked throughout. If the themes are not disturbing to you I can wholeheartedly recommend reading it.
