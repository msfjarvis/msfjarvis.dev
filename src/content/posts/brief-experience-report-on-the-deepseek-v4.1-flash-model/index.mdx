Quick round of disclaimers: This is not going to be exhaustive, I didn't compare them against exactly identical tasks, and this blog post is effectively me turning a somewhat wordy journal entry into a more suitable format. Take it with an appropriate amount of salt as per your dietary needs.

I've recently been burning Codex tokens at both ends (relatively speaking, I could never rival "real" vibe coders) testing out the famous [Matt Pocock skills](https://github.com/mattpocock/skills) package to rebuild a [slop tool](https://git.msfjarvis.dev/msfjarvis/actual-budget-backfiller) that had achieved slop singularity and become such a mess not even the clankers could figure it out anymore. I'll probably write a separate thing about that process (early hint: I get why it has 260K stars) but this post is mostly about the swap from Codex to DeepSeek. For access to DeepSeek models, I signed up for [OpenRouter](https://openrouter.ai) and paid them $10 for an initial top-up of credits.

I had already built around 70-80% of [factual-budget](https://git.msfjarvis.dev/msfjarvis/factual-budget/) with Codex (last pre-DeepSeek commit [is this](https://git.msfjarvis.dev/msfjarvis/factual-budget/src/commit/3a7ed81dd749a22354df4e74635c5e138133fd50), and swapped to DeepSeek to implement the final feature of writing out transactions to Actual Budget. I haven't figured out how to share the session transcript in its nice-ish HTML format yet, but 🔜™️

## Major differences

- DeepSeek models are **thonkers**. I initially [set up DeepSeek](https://git.msfjarvis.dev/msfjarvis/my-pi/commit/b9d27124e255a463a92a521a5fb5a4004a2c3cf1) with the `high` reasoning effort I use with other models but the unredacted reasoning going on for aeons started ticking me off and I [switched reasoning to `off`](https://git.msfjarvis.dev/msfjarvis/my-pi/commit/50e5e57ad3b6d5f34b9db83cd2f3d3f0e84f9743), not that it helped...
- They seem extremely allergic to tool calls. Maybe it's something with Pi or my specific setup? The LLM consistently chooses to invoke `cat`, `sed`, and `find` when my system prompt includes specific guidance to use the harness-provided tools instead of shelling out.
- The inference speeds were comparable to OpenAI at the start of the session, but were much more unstable as the session reached around the 150K token mark.
- The cost difference is simply unreal. I used Ope
