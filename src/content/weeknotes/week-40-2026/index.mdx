# Social

[Fenil](https://fknil.com) pitched [Yash](https://yashgarg.dev) and me on a breakfast place nearby, except we chose to go there on Tuesday. Guess the one day in the week they are closed :)

To celebrate Cloudflare's 16th birthday, we had a team dinner at the Hilton hotel in Embassy GolfLinks. It was nice to chat with everyone again after nearly a month of not having an office. We were given a soft promise of the new office reopening some time in the next 5 to 10 days 🤞. I'm eagerly looking forward to getting some time in there before I leave for home at the end of the month.

Following up on the promise to myself [made during the last IndieWebClub](/weeknotes/week-38-2026/), I chose to go a little early to hang out with people; I had a great time yapping with [Ankur](https://ankursethi.com/) and a few others who shall go unnamed purely because I am terrible with names 😓

The discussions at IWC itself were quite spirited and engaging. Apparently we all have a lot of strong opinions on archiving! Could be fun to have another session dedicated purely towards it. I ended up using the writing time to _delete_ some drafts instead of working on them, so admittedly the spirit of that exercise was a little lost on me.

## Movies

Yash and I watched two movies this week: [Digger](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt31450459/) starring Tom Cruise and [Primetime](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt34206385/) starring Robert Pattinson.

Digger was deeply weird and often hard to follow, but Tom Cruise really delivers something unique here that we haven't seen in a long, long while from him. I could go for a re-watch of this _today_ and still have as much fun as I did.

Primetime is significantly less _weird_, but delivers just as much on the acting front. Robert Pattinson has proved time and again that he's a phenomenal actor and this was no different. His Chris Hansen is deeply troubled, an obvious hypocrite, and just as fucked up as someone trying to convert paedophilia into TV views would be. Aftermath had an in-depth [breakdown](https://aftermath.site/primetime-hansen-review-impressions) of the movie that I quite enjoyed reading and resonated with me.

## Games

[CONTROL: Resonant](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3669870/CONTROL_Resonant/) still has me fully hooked, and I've been making steady progress on it all week. I got through two Resonant fights; the Factory Resonant fight was extremely telegraphed and barely had anything to it, so I one-shot it with ease. On my final attempt I was so locked in that I forgot to blink and my eyes started burning to remind me of the fact. I need to take this stuff less seriously!

# Personal Projects

Somewhat unsurprisingly, last week's [Claw release](https://github.com/msfjarvis/compose-lobsters/releases/tag/v1.72.0) got rejected by the [IzzyOnDroid](https://izzyondroid.org/) repository for its new proprietary dependency on [Play Core](https://developer.android.com/guide/playcore), so I had to create a separate [FOSS-only build](https://github.com/msfjarvis/compose-lobsters/pull/1291) to satisfy their requirements again. The team behind IzzyOnDroid do great work on extending the core [F-Droid](https://f-droid.org) repository with apps that are still FOSS but might have some other properties that make them unsuitable for core. I've donated to them in the past and would encourage others to [do the same](https://izzyondroid.org/contributing/Donating/)!

This site received some very minor tweaks; footnotes now render as [popovers](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/Popover_API) on the website (like this[^1]), the dark theme for code blocks was changed, and the WebMentions server was tweaked to make it possible to provide per-host renderers so I can tweak how, say, [bubbles.town](https://bubbles.town/) webmentions show up under my posts.
