# Personal projects

Busy week for [Claw](https://github.com/msfjarvis/compose-lobsters/), my Android app for the [Lobsters link aggregator community](https://lobste.rs) as I shipped fixes for a multitude of UI and UX bugs. [v1.74.0](https://github.com/msfjarvis/compose-lobsters/releases/tag/v1.74.0) includes a completely new HTML renderer (used to render comments) which fixes some longstanding bugs <sup>[[1](https://github.com/cbeyls/HtmlConverterCompose/issues/26)][[2](https://github.com/cbeyls/HtmlConverterCompose/issues/20)]</sup>. It also restores proper support for Android's new [predictive back animations](https://developer.android.com/design/ui/mobile/guides/patterns/predictive-back) which had silently regressed as part of a dependency update some time ago.

As part of this I also discovered that [Tramline](https://tramline.app/), the release automation platform I've used for Claw for the past 2-3 years had actually wound down commercial operations. It is now being run by a couple of its original maintainers, so I naturally signed up as a GitHub sponsor to help keep the lights on. If you have mobile apps and hate having to interact with the App Stores, you should sign up too! I've never had a single problem with their platform and this "shout out" is mostly because they deserve it for the immense amount of value the service has provided to me for free.

# Cooking

Very great week for cooking enjoyers on this here blog as we made many new things and flubbed our way through all of it.

We made biryani this week, and only discovered midway through that we had no Biryani masala in the house. The magic of quick commerce was called upon, where I accidentally ordered a "biryani kit" that you're supposed to drop rice and chicken into while we already had a full masala base and everything in place. I suspect that contributed to why it tasted so damn amazing.

Other than that we also had a day of air frying some chicken breasts, making simple vegetable curries, and having yet another comedy of errors while making an egg curry. [Yash](https://yashgarg.dev/) only _soft_-boiled the eggs which made peeling them an absolute pain, and we lost one soldier in the ensuing struggle. Then we realized the recipe we were following was written by some demon who wanted us to use 2 _tablespoons_ of chilli powder. The entire thing was looking positively radioactive by the end so we decided to add a bit of heavy cream to salvage it, and we were rewarded with edible dinner.
