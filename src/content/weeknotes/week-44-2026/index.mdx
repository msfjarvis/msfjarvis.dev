# Personal Projects

With completely perfect timing Lobsters made a markup change that broke my app while I was on my flight back and was not going to have access to a PC for the rest of the day. I unsuccessfully tried having Copilot take a stab at it then just took the hit and filed an issue so people knew I was aware. The release fixing it [went out today](https://github.com/msfjarvis/compose-lobsters/releases/tag/v1.76.0).

My PC was fully losing its mind as I tried to unpack and boot it up, with things crashing left and right and system stability generally being in the gutter. It took me a full day of struggling before I saw a log line about an invalid memory reference and it all clicked: once again I've been cursed with bad RAM. A quick `memtest86+` run confirmed that there indeed are a lot of errors, which ruined my night rather proper. As a hail Mary I decided to rerun the test with just one stick which passed, so I will at least have 16 gigs of RAM in the worst case. I'll take some time to test the other stick in isolation and rejig around the slots hoping I can get both of them working, but chances are rather slim. My exact 16G x 2 RAM kit currently costs 65K on Amazon so this is about the second worst thing that could've broken down after the NVIDIA GPU.

The irony of LLM usage right after that paragraph is not lost on me.
