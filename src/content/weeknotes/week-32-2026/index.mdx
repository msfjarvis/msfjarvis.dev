I decided to do some window shopping on [pi.dev/packages](https://pi.dev/packages) and picked up [pi-spark](https://pi.dev/packages/pi-spark), and [pi-codex-conversion](https://pi.dev/packages/@howaboua/pi-codex-conversion?name=pi-codex). My attempt to use the voice input feature failed which honestly I'm happier to not have. # Cooking

Lots of cooking at home this week despite a string of sickness, which was very helpful for my very aching wallet. I was alone at home on Monday, so I made a small batch of veggie pasta and called it there. Next couple of days we had chhole, after which I got sick and opted to order in a chicken soup. On Friday we made a quick shakshouka, and I was alone again on Saturday so a veggie pasta with some low fat nachos were my dinner. I was craving something sweet right after that, so I tried making the "protein" staple balls of oats/banana/peanut butter/protein powder/honey. I didn't quite nail the consistency, and they were a little too wet. I opted to stuff it into a glass box and eat them as bars the next day instead, and it was still not quite firm but a great snack regardless.
