# Cooking

Lots of homecooked meals this week which made me quite happy, some pictured below and the others not. We unlocked a new failure mode in the kitchen this week of "adding double the required amount". The chana paneer gravy recipe turned into a paste because it had twice the paneer than it was supposed to. We were having trouble blending the paneer and didn't clock why until everything was in the pan. Actual comedy of errors stuff, but at least it still came out tasty.
