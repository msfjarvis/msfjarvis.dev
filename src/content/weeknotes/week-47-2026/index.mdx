After a relaxing 3 weeks at home I'm finally back in Bengaluru. Having moved out for a little over 18 months at this point I've started thinking of Ghaziabad as my parents' house and Bengaluru as my home which is a weird feeling to reckon with given how much time I've spent close to and in that place.

# Personal

I had a bear of a time getting my Singapore visa application through, partly through my own carelessness and partly due to delays from the agency but after a week of back and forth all the documents are with them now. Hopefully it'll get approved quickly like it was for the rest of the group and I can put that behind me.

In an even bigger win I managed to make Mother Dearest part with her 20 year old kitchen appliances and replaced 2 broken-in-different-ways mixers with a nice [Kuuvings one](https://www.amazon.in/dp/B0FNQS4RRD) which Yash and I have here in Bengaluru. She's a fan so far 😄

After some scheduling difficulties on my very last day in Ghaziabad I was able to meet up with my childhood friend Aryan. My parents also wanted to meet him so we made a date night out of it for them, they dropped me off so I could chat for a few hours while they shopped around and had a nice dinner then met Aryan on our way back. It was a fun night for the lot of us.

I've slowly but surely trying to get my very old school mother to learn to decouple her happiness with whatever the fuck her children are up to, and small things like this are warming her up to the idea. They went on a trip to Odisha earlier this year and according to their own words it was probably the first time there were no arguments. The reason is obviously the lack of the two bumbling children regardless of what they try to tell me :)

### Confusing brain things sidebar

On the flight back while we were buying refreshments from the on-board pantry, the person next to me had their cards rejected and didn't have enough cash either so they had to go (probably) hungry. I had a working card and wanted to offer to pay for them, but I spent so long mulling over how the interaction would go and how to not sound like a patronizing asshole that the moment passed and I ended up doing nothing. I kept running that back in my head for most of that flight and could not place what exactly was I so afraid of in that moment which made me not help out this person. What made me so hesitant in engaging with a stranger for what I felt was a very basic act of kindness? It's very frustrating that I barely have a grasp on my own brain's tendencies and froze up in such a low stakes situation where I could've been of help to someone.
