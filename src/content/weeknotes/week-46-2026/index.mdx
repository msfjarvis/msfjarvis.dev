# Personal Projects

[Claw](https://github.com/msfjarvis/compose-lobsters) had a bunch of changes this week. I [reorganized the entire codebase](https://github.com/msfjarvis/compose-lobsters/compare/709f4c9c4d1015aa96b9953fcc479f1fa44d7b49...5cc1981eb7028e8795a8b2b54780664914824022) to more closely follow the Android multi-module guidance, going from 10 modules to 37 as of writing. The previous module layout was inherited from the app's [past as Kotlin Multiplatform app](https://github.com/msfjarvis/compose-lobsters/tree/archive) which had become messy and cumbersome in the 5 years since. It's also a chance for me to get some practical experience with taming a decently sized Gradle build (relatively speaking, Cash App's has 800+ modules) and learn how things like compilation avoidance and cache invalidation work in these scenarios.

I also cut out the [Haze](https://github.com/chrisbanes/haze) backed fancy navigation bar and replaced it with the [standard Material3 one](https://github.com/msfjarvis/compose-lobsters/commit/a8788f433e5ca2416404d9bf5c64fee001cb3f07).

Another UI change that landed this week was adding a small [shared element transition](https://developer.android.com/develop/ui/compose/animation/shared-elements) going from a post to its comments page, which uncovered [a regression](https://issuetracker.google.com/issues/585614999) in the Navigation3 library.

Some homelab changes: I swapped out my webmail from [alps](https://git.sr.ht/~migadu/alps) to [pl_mail](https://github.com/karatektus/pl_mail), and quite love it. It supports WebDAV + CalDAV, SSO, and just works much better in general. There are obvious downsides in the form of PHP and it being complete slop, but alps is just as bad so I've made peace with the fact that selfhosted webmail can either be ancient or slop. To minimize risk I've kept pl_mail in its containerized format instead of directly running it on the host, and kept it restricted to my [Tailscale](https://tailscale.com/) network.
