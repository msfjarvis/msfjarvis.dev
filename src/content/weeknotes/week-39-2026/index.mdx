## 🤖CLANK CLANK CLANK 🤖

I've been burning tokens left and right on objectively pointless stuff, with the new OpenAI models being as cheap as they are. I recently discovered that [Nixpkgs](https://github.com/NixOS/nixpkgs) archives support being compressed as `.tar.zst` files instead of just `.tar.gz` and they come down to 20% smaller. I tried naively hosting a static archive but that doesn't work for reproducibility, and there's a proper [specification for it](https://nix.dev/manual/nix/2.25/protocols/tarball-fetcher) explaining how to do it. I went back to the stack of Matt Pocock's skills which I have come to like a lot, and managed to quickly implement a [Cloudflare Worker](https://git.msfjarvis.dev/msfjarvis/acceptable-vibes/src/branch/main/nix-channels-worker) that stores tarballs in R2 and correctly implements the Lockable HTTP Tarball protocol.
