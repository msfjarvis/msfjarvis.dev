[Prettier](https://prettier.io) is a JavaScript-based formatter that I use for this very site's codebase, since it is quite extensible and can handle a wide variety of formats. I recently noticed a specific quirk with it where you need to specifically pre-format JSX in a certain way to get a specific output from it. With this source document and no formatting configured:

```mdx
<Figure
  src={lego_dino_onesie}
  alt="A large red LEGO character wearing a dino onesie holding a small dinosaur egg"
/>

<Gallery>

    <Figure
        src={lego_dino_onesie}
        alt="A large red LEGO character wearing a dino onesie holding a small dinosaur egg"
    />

    <Figure
        src={lego_spiderman}
        alt="A LEGO Spider-Man figure displayed among other models"
    />

</Gallery>
```

Notice how the outer Figure is now 2 space indented but the inner ones didn't change? Here's how you need to _manually_ lay out the inner text for Prettier to format it like the other one:

```mdx
<Figure
  src={lego_dino_onesie}
  alt="A large red LEGO character wearing a dino onesie holding a small dinosaur egg"
/>

<Gallery>
  <Figure
    src={lego_dino_onesie}
    alt="A large red LEGO character wearing a dino onesie holding a small dinosaur egg"
  />
  <Figure
    src={lego_spiderman}
    alt="A LEGO Spider-Man figure displayed among other models"
  />
</Gallery>
```
